Commercial expansion roadmap
Scale through trust and control.

The next product steps prioritize dependable data, collaborative governance, and automation that remains subject to human approval.

Foundation

Data reliability

  • Reusable CSV/XLSX mapping profiles and industry data dictionaries
  • Domain validation rules and outlier diagnostics
  • Forecast accuracy monitoring outside the training sample
Enterprise workflow

Collaboration at scale

  • Multi-site scenario comparison and version-aware audit records
  • Role-based access, approvals, and accountable change history
  • Controlled connectors for operational and workforce systems
Decision augmentation

Human-approved automation

  • Cost, SLA, utilization, and risk as explicit multi-objective trade-offs
  • Explainable analyst assistance grounded in authorized scenario data
  • Recommendations that require an accountable operating approval
Product principle: Every recommendation must remain explainable, reproducible, auditable, and controlled by the organization that acts on it.
TRUSTED DATA → GOVERNED COLLABORATION → HUMAN-APPROVED ACTION
