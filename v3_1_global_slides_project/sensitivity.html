Sensitivity analysis
Expose risk before it becomes disruption.

QueueCraft tests demand and service-time uncertainty around the baseline, then compares expected delay and tail-risk exposure under the same Monte Carlo conditions.

How to read the scenario grid

01 · Demand

Move across the columns

Each step raises or lowers arrival demand relative to the baseline operating case.

02 · Service

Move down the rows

Each step changes service duration to reveal the effect of slower or faster throughput.

03 · Risk

Use mean and P95 together

Expected wait describes typical exposure; P95 highlights the unfavorable but plausible operating tail.

Decision use: identify the high-risk cells, then choose a deliberate capacity buffer rather than staffing only for the average case.
