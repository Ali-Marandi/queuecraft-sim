## Product implication

QueueCraft should compete initially as an offline-first decision-support and simulation studio rather than as a visitor-facing queue-management SaaS. Its strongest differentiation is transparent, reproducible, auditable scenario analysis with local data control. The commercial roadmap should therefore prioritize signed scenario packages, role-aware workspaces, calibrated historical-data import, multi-objective optimization, report templates, and reviewed read-only connectors before attempting full real-time digital-twin operation.
