## Slide 1

### Operational decisions fail when data and risk are disconnected

- Queue models are only as reliable as their input data and operating assumptions.
- QueueCraft now connects validated time-series data to repeatable, risk-aware capacity analysis.
- The result is a decision record that links source quality, scenario, assumptions, and recommendation.

## Slide 2

### Data quality becomes a first-class operational control

- Import CSV/XLSX event data or aggregated arrival counts through a native desktop workflow.
- Detect invalid timestamps, invalid or negative counts, duplicate timestamps, and missing time buckets.
- Surface an explicit quality score, warnings, preview, and chronology before the model is run.

## Slide 3

### Sensitivity makes uncertainty visible before it becomes disruption

- Test demand and service-time shifts around the baseline with a common Monte Carlo seed.
- Compare expected and P95 waiting time across nine operating conditions.
- Use the heatmap to identify resilience gaps and choose the capacity buffer deliberately.

## Slide 4

### Pareto analysis replaces one-size-fits-all staffing recommendations

- Evaluate candidate staffing allocations against two competing objectives: server cost and end-to-end waiting time.
- Remove dominated plans, retain non-dominated alternatives, and make trade-offs visible.
- Recommend the least-cost SLA-compliant option or explicitly flag a residual SLA risk.

## Slide 5

### Global-ready by design, not by translation afterthought

- English and Persian language packs, persistent language selection, and RTL-ready presentation are built into the desktop experience.
- Locally bundled assets and local scenario storage support controlled, offline-capable environments.
- Scenario fingerprints, repeatable seeds, and explicit SLA assessments create an auditable operational record.

## Slide 6

### Commercial expansion is focused on trust, collaboration, and scale

- Enterprise expansion: multi-site collaboration, role-based access control, approval workflows, and integration connectors.
